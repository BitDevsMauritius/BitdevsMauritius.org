Topics

Lightning Network — Daemons & Onion Messaging

Duration: 1 Hour

Speakers: Hanif & Sadiq

Meetup Agenda

1. Opening & Context (5 minutes)

Speaker: Hanif

  • Welcome and introduction
  • Overview of BitDevs Mauritius and The Blockchain Society ALCHE collaboration
  • Why the Lightning Network matters for Bitcoin scalability and privacy

2. Understanding Lightning Daemons (20 minutes)

Speaker: Sadiq

Objective: Explore the architecture behind Lightning nodes

  • What Lightning daemons are
  • Overview of popular implementations (LND, Core Lightning, Eclair)
  • Node communication and channel management
  • How daemons handle payments and routing
  • The role of peers, channels, and network connectivity

3. Onion Messaging & Routing (25 minutes)

Speaker: Hanif

Objective: Understand privacy and communication in Lightning

  • Introduction to onion routing
  • How onion messages travel across the network
  • Privacy preservation in Lightning payments
  • HTLCs and secure payment forwarding
  • Trade-offs and limitations in routing design

Discussion:

  • Real-world implications for scalability and privacy
  • Why onion routing matters in decentralized systems

4. Open Discussion & Closing (10 minutes)

Speakers: Hanif & Sadiq

  • Questions and community discussion
  • Recommended resources for further exploration
  • Preview of future BitDevs Mauritius sessions
  • Btrust Builders
