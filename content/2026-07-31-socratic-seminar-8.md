# Topics

### Lightning Network Fundamentals & Builder Pathways

**Duration:** 1 Hour

**Speakers:** Hanif & Tooshar

---
Meetup Agenda

**1. Welcome & Introduction (5 minutes)**

**Speaker:** Tooshar

- Welcome and meetup overview
- Recap of the previous Lightning Network session
- Objectives for today's discussion

**2. Lightning Network Fundamentals (25 minutes)**

**Speaker:** Hanif

*Objective: Reinforce core Lightning concepts and answer outstanding questions.*

*Topics covered:*
- Review of payment channels
- Lightning daemon architecture
- Onion routing and message propagation
- Routing payments across the network
- Interactive Q&A and concept clarification

**3. Builder Pathways: From Learner to Contributor (20 minutes)**

**Speaker:** Tooshar

*Objective: Highlight practical pathways into Bitcoin development.*

*Topics covered:*
- Introduction to the Btrust Builder Pathway
- Personal journey into the Builder Pathway
- Lessons learned from studying Mastering Bitcoin
- Advice for new developers starting their Bitcoin journey
- Open-source contribution opportunities

**4. Community Discussion & Closing (10 minutes)**

**Speakers:** Hanif & Tooshar

- Open discussion and questions
- Resources for continued learning
- Preview of next month's BitDevs Mauritius meetup
- Community announcements and networking
