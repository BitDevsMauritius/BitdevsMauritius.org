# Topics

### BIP-110, Bitcoin Governance & Lightning series

**Duration:** 1 Hour

**Speaker:** Sadiq

---
Meetup Agenda

**1. Opening & Context (5 minutes)**

**Speaker:** Sadiq

- What is happening in Bitcoin development right now?
- Introduction to BIP-110
- Why governance and consensus matter

**2. BIP-110 Deep Dive (25 minutes)**

**Speaker:** Sadiq

- What BIP-110 proposes
- The technical changes involved
- Consensus rules and activation
- The roles of Bitcoin Core developers, miners, node operators, and users
- What happens when the ecosystem disagrees
- Socratic discussion: Who actually controls Bitcoin?

**3. Lightning Network — Continuing the Series (20 minutes)**

**Speaker:** Sadiq

- Quick recap of previous Lightning concepts
- Connecting Lightning to Bitcoin's base-layer rules
- Deeper look at onion messaging and routing
- How Lightning continues to evolve on top of Bitcoin
- Open questions from previous sessions

**4. Discussion & Closing (10 minutes)**

**Speaker:** Sadiq

- Community discussion around BIP-110 and Lightning
- Key takeaways
- Questions and follow-up topics
- Preview of the next BitDevs session
